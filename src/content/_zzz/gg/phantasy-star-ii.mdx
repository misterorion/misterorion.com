There was a time in around 2008-2010 that I couldn't stop thinking about this game. I think it was because I never completed it, and it was the first Sega Genesis game that I never completed.

_Phantasy Star II_ was released for the Sega Genesis in 1990. As a youth in love with _Phantasy Star_ on the Master System, I followed the sequel closely in magazines such as _Game Pro_.

When I finally got my hands on this very expensive sequel and started playing for the first time, I distinctly recall a feeling of disappointment.

It lacked the "3D" dungeons of the first installment, and monster encounters were staged in a sterile grid, unlike the colorful and relatively realistic backdrops of the original.

<Figure

And it was brutally difficult. Dungeons were grueling, multi-level affairs, laden with traps, fall-through chutes and poison floors. The North American release included a hint book because it was believed that this audience, less exposed to punishing JRPGs and "grinding" mechanics, needed some hand holding.

<Figure
    src="/phantasy-star-ii/paseo.png"
    alt="Battle"
/>

I did enjoy the "cinematic" dialog, the setting featuring a neat mix of science fiction and medieval (like the original). The music was also a high point, and several tracks are still on my playlists.

I never did beat _Phantasy Star II_ but the experience it represented, of disappointment, like the game itself, was a first for me.
