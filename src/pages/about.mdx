# Hey

I'm a senior cloud infrastructure engineer with over 6 years building production-grade infrastructure from the ground up. Most recently at [Aescape][1], I've been architecting AWS and Kubernetes environments that support high-traffic APIs and data pipelines. I've led SOC2 compliance efforts, built GitOps workflows, and mentored engineers while staying deeply hands-on with infrastructure as code.

Previously, I was a Technical Director and DevOps Engineer at [Havas Production Studios][2], managing builds and deployment pipelines in both AWS (CodePipeline) and on-prem (Jenkins), wielding Python, Bash, and PowerShell to smooth the development process. Prior to this, I was a sysadmin and email developer.
